I presented the [Document Digital Signature](https://docs.google.com/open?id=0B3l05SpMzl27dmlhUU05M1NzZnc) profile to the S&I Framework - esMD workgroup. This group is looking for ways  to have non-repudiation proof of authorship and are hell-bent on using digital signatures. I am not convinced that they yet have the compelling use-case to overcome the 'costs' that come along with [Document Digital Signatures](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2011/06/ihe-privacy-and-security-profiles.html). These costs are money, but more so they are resources, changes to workflows, and a long-term commitment. It is  this long-term commitment that usually stops most projects short.
