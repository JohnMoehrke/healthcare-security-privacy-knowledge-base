I [presented](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2011/09/preparing-for-iec-80001-security.html) the soon to be published Security Technical Report IEC 80001-2-2 "Guidance for the disclosure and communication of medical device security needs, risks and controls". There is an assumption that the audience has an understanding of the basics of IEC 80001-1 "Application of Risk Management for IT-Networks Incorporating Medical Devices". I have been involved in the creation of this set of specifications from the beginning and provided [some background here on my blog](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2010/11/iec-80001-risk-assessment-to-be-used.html).

This webinar was well attended for a security webinar with close to 150 participants. My blog has also been very actively referenced since then. So I can tell there is strong interest. Unfortunately the audio recording of the webinar failed, so the best I can do is offer up the slides.
