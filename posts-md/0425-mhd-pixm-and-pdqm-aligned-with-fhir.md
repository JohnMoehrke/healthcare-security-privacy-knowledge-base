This is the [notice of Ballot](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!topic/ititech/7xjDcyGtvhQ) on the updated Profiles from IHE on MHD, PDQm, and PIXm. This ballot to review and approve the updates so that they can be formally tested at EU Connectathon. Please review, and provide constructive comments.

In general these profiles are thinner than previous as the FHIR specification in DSTU2 is far more readable and complete. Thus there is less description and 'profiling' that IHE needs to do. As such these IHE profiles rely more on the good readability of the FHIR specification in DSTU2.

[Other Blog articles on FHIR Topic](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/p/topics.html#FHIR)
