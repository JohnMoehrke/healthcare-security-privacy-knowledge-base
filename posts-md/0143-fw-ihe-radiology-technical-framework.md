**IHE Radiology Technical Framework Supplements Published for Public Comment**

The IHE Radiology Technical Committee has published the following supplements to the IHE Radiology Technical Framework for Public Comment in the period from ****March 30 through April 30, 2012****:

- [Cross-Enterprise Document Reliable Interchange of Images (XDR-I)](http://ihe.us1.list-manage2.com/track/click?u=a75e449ea24da34a9a89ed37a&id=cf6f61fc9c&e=494a372b78)

- [Import Reconciliation Workflow Extensions (IRWF Extensions)](http://ihe.us1.list-manage1.com/track/click?u=a75e449ea24da34a9a89ed37a&id=31871dfc45&e=494a372b78)

- [Post-Processing Workflow with Application Hosting (PPWF.b)](http://ihe.us1.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a75e449ea24da34a9a89ed37a&id=625905a442&e=494a372b78)

These profiles will be available for testing at subsequent IHE Connectathons. Comments submitted by ****April 30, 2012**** will be considered by the Radiology Technical Committee in developing the trial implementation version of the supplements. The documents are available for download at [http://ihe.net/Technical_Framework/public_comment.cfm](http://ihe.us1.list-manage2.com/track/click?u=a75e449ea24da34a9a89ed37a&id=4c320fc470&e=494a372b78). Comments should be submitted at [http://ihe.net/radiology/radiologycomments.cfm](http://ihe.us1.list-manage2.com/track/click?u=a75e449ea24da34a9a89ed37a&id=1323e4f5ae&e=494a372b78).
