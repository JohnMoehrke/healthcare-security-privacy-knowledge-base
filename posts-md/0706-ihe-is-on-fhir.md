This was just reported in an[IHE-Europe news letter](https://mailchi.mp/ihe-europe/connectathon_2021_online_00?e=d9a069fb32). I wanted to stress it here as I am finding many people do not understand that IHE has many Implementation Guides (IHE-Profiles) that leverage FHIR. [IHE has published 32 Implementation Guides that use FHIR](https://wiki.ihe.net/index.php/Category:FHIR).

There is very active implementation and testing of these at IHE-Connectathons. This graphic brings the message home
