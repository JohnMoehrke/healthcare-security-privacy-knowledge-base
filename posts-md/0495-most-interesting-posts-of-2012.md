A short tutorial on [How to apply Risk Assessment to get your Security and Privacy Requirements](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2012/03/how-to-apply-risk-assessment-to-get.html), Explain how an HIE can [Enforce Security and Privacy Policy in a XDS Registry](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2012/03/policy-enforcing-xds-registry.html), Finally see [Meaningful Use Stage 2 means Secure and Privacy Protecting](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2012/03/meaningful-use-stage-2-finally-means.html), Again explain the [the benefit of an HIE](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2012/03/what-is-benefit-of-hie.html), [Stepping stone off of FAX to Secure-Email](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2012/03/stepping-stone-off-of-fax-to-secure.html),  [Meaningful Use Stage 2 -- 170.202 Transport](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2012/03/meaningful-use-stage-2-170202-transport.html), [Meaningful Use Stage 2 seems to support Security](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2012/03/meaningful-use-stage-2-security-privacy.html), [Huge HIE -- the Care Continuity Consortium](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2012/03/huge-hie-care-continuity-consortium.html), [Healthcare is not secure - trust suffers](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2012/03/healthcare-is-not-secure-trust-suffers.html), and announce more [IHE - ITI NEW Educational Webinar Series](http://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/2012/03/ihe-iti-new-educational-webinar-series.html)
