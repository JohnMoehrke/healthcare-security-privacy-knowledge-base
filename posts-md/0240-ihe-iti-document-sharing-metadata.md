IHE IT Infrastructure Handbook Published for Public Comment

The IHE IT Infrastructure Technical Committee has published the following new handbook for public comment in the period from June 13 through July 13, 2018:

- Document Sharing Metadata - Rev. 1.0

The document is available for download at [http://ihe.net/Public_Comment](https://ihe.us1.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a75e449ea24da34a9a89ed37a&id=343621364d&e=f71f998cbe). Comments submitted by July 13, 2018 will be considered by the IHE IT Infrastructure Technical Committee in developing the subsequent version of the handbook. Comments can be submitted at [ITI Public Comments](https://ihe.us1.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a75e449ea24da34a9a89ed37a&id=89a247d5ed&e=f71f998cbe).
