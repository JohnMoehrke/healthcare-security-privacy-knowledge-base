I was made aware of an open-source project that has addressed converting classic DICOM Audit Messages into the new FHIR AuditEvent. This open-source project is [managed on this github repository](https://github.com/upmc-enterprises/ATNA2FHIR). I helped the authors with some background and understanding, but I take no credit for the work they have done.

I am sure they welcome comments, suggestions, and assistance.

My blog [articles on Audit logging for Security, Privacy, and others](https://healthcaresecprivacy.blogspot.com/p/topics.html#Audit)
