I have written many articles on the US Centric - Meaningful Use Stage 2. Some are very deep analysis of specific problems. It may seem that all I have to say is negative and nitpicky things. I want to make it clear that I am very happy with how Stage 2 came out. I just find that others have done a fantastic job of outlining the rules, so I have focused only on deep analysis in my area of expertise where I see potential confusion.

First, the [ONC and CMS summaries are great stuff](http://healthit.hhs.gov/portal/server.pt?open=512&mode=2&objID=3584). They do leave out details that end up  being the subject of my blog articles. But they are really good summaries. [Keith has clarified](http://motorcycleguy.blogspot.com/2012/03/on-meaningfuluse-stage2-proposed-rules.html) much as well. I have seen plenty other summaries, I wish I had a catalog. There is just no good reason for me to pile on.
