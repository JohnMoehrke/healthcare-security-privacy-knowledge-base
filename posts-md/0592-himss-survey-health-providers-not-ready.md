This should be no surprise. As much as I would love for the results to be better, I think that this represents the perceived risk vs cost.

> Many healthcare organizations are not prepared to meet tougher privacy and security terms contained in the health IT stimulus law, according to a survey by the Healthcare Information and Management Systems Society. The HITECH provisions of the law strengthened penalties for mishandling personal health information by providers. [More](http://govhealthit.com/newsitem.aspx?nid=72325)
