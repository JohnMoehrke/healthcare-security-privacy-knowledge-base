This is a case of a Doctor believing that the tool he used protected  the data. This seems to me a case where making something too easy  results in failure. The fortunate thing is that it is unlikely anyone  else noticed.

> The information was available so long because the U of L  doctor who  set up the Web site thought the information was protected by  a password  and other precautions, Hebert said."It was just a mistake,"  Hebert said. "It was his  understanding it was password protected."
