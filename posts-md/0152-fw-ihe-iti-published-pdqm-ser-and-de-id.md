IHE IT Infrastructure Technical Framework Supplements Published for Public Comment

The IHE IT Infrastructure Technical Committee has published the following supplements to the IHE IT Infrastructure Technical Framework for public comment in the period from **June 6 through July 5, 2014**:

- Patient Demographics Query for Mobile (PDQm)

- Secure Retrieve (SeR)

The documents are available for download at [http://www.ihe.net/Public_Comment/](http://ihe.us1.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a75e449ea24da34a9a89ed37a&id=85515ed7f8&e=96e0d93dc1). Comments submitted by **July 5, 2014** will be considered by the IHE IT Infrastructure Technical Committee in developing the trial implementation versions of the supplements. Comments can be submitted at [http://ihe.net/ITI_Public_Comments/](http://ihe.us1.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a75e449ea24da34a9a89ed37a&id=22d4994045&e=96e0d93dc1).

The committee has also published the following Handbook:

- De-Identification

- De-Identification Mapping (Excel file)

The documents are available for download at [http://ihe.net/Technical_Frameworks/](http://ihe.us1.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a75e449ea24da34a9a89ed37a&id=c493e5b4ad&e=96e0d93dc1). Comments on all documents are invited at any time and can be submitted at [http://ihe.net/ITI_Public_Comments/](http://ihe.us1.list-manage.com/track/click?u=a75e449ea24da34a9a89ed37a&id=60e7c558aa&e=96e0d93dc1).
